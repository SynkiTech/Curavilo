   550→   550→# 11. IDOR / BOLA PROTECTION
   551→   551→
   552→   552→Treat every object-ID endpoint as a potential Broken Object Level Authorization vulnerability.
   553→   553→
   554→   554→For every endpoint/action:
   555→   555→
   556→   556→```text id="5e4v4b"
   557→   557→GET /patients/:id
   558→   558→GET /reports/:id
   559→   559→GET /invoices/:id
   560→   560→GET /files/:id
   561→   561→POST /payments/:id/refund
   562→   562→PATCH /staff/:id
   563→   563→DELETE /workflow/:id
   564→   564→```
   565→   565→
   566→   566→verify object authorization server-side.
   567→   567→
   568→   568→Do NOT rely on:
   569→   569→
   570→   570→* hidden UI elements
   571→   571→* UUID unpredictability
   572→   572→* route protection alone
   573→   573→* client-side role checks
